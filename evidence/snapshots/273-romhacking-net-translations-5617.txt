Game Description:

999: Nine Hours, Nine Persons, Nine Doors is a visual novel adventure video game written by Kotaro Uchikoshi and developed by Chunsoft. The story is about a college student, named Junpei who is abducted along with eight other people and forced to play the “Nonary Game” which puts its participants in a life or death situation, to escape from a sinking cruise liner.

Translation Description:

Arabic translation patch for the Nintendo DS game “999: 9 Hours, 9 Persons, 9 Doors”.

This translation project officially started in 2016 after figuring out how to make the text flow from right to left, and after 4 long years the time has finally come for Arabs to enjoy this masterpiece of a game.

Please do note that the at6p encrypted graphics are not translated but may be added in a future update if the means to decrypt them is found.
