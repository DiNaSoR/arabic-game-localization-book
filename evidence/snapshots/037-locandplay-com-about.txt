A Game Loc Studio

Loc & Play is a game localization studio specializing in Arabic with all of its dialects, Turkish, and other regional languages. We don’t just translate games. We adapt, voice, test, and tune every piece of your story until it clicks with Arab and Middle Eastern players.

In the Heart of the Middle East

We’re proudly local — with two offices in UAE and Egypt — and we work closely with both regional partners and global publishers. Being here, in the region, gives us the context and player insight global teams often miss.

Beyond linguists — we’re gamers

The people behind our localization, LQA, and testing are players who get every puzzle, joke, pun, and plot twist.

We specialize in Arabic voice-over and dubbing. Whether it’s modern standard Arabic or regional dialects (Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine), our voice teams bring emotion and energy that fits the tone of the game — not just the words.

A Decade in the Game

We’ve been doing this for over 10 years, supported by the experience, reputation, and resources of our parent company Loc & More — one of the most respected localization brands in the region.
