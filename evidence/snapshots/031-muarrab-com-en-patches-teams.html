Common questions

Who is الحلم المتجدد للتعريب?

An amateur yet professional team specializing in game localization, aiming to break down cultural barriers and deliver language experiences that do justice to both Arabic and the original dialogue.

How many games has الحلم المتجدد للتعريب translated?

121 games on Muarrab. The most visited: Hollow Knight, Bloodborne and Dark Souls III.

Are الحلم المتجدد للتعريب's translations free?

Yes, all of its translations on Muarrab are free.

Which platforms do الحلم المتجدد للتعريب's translations run on?

PC (84), PlayStation 4 (20), Nintendo Switch (19), NES (12), Game Boy Advance (5), Super Nintendo (5), Android (4), Nintendo DS (2), Sega Master System (2), Sega Mega Drive (2), Game Boy Color (1), PlayStation Portable (1), Nintendo GameCube (1), Game Boy (1), PlayStation 1 (1), Wii U (1) and Nintendo 3DS (1).

Where can I download الحلم المتجدد للتعريب's translations?

Open the game's page on Muarrab to find a link to the team's official download page, along with the translation type and supported platforms. Team website: etrdream.com.

How is الحلم المتجدد للتعريب's rating calculated?

It's the average of Muarrab visitors' ratings of الحلم المتجدد للتعريب's translations. Visitors rate the translation of a specific game from this page, one rating per translation, which they can change later.
